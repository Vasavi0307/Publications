## 1. Altered neuroepithelial morphogenesis and migration defects in iPSC-derived cerebral organoids and 2D neural stem cells in familial bipolar disorder

**Oxford Open Neuroscience**, 2024, kvae007 · DOI: [10.1093/oons/kvae007](https://doi.org/10.1093/oons/kvae007)

**Authors:** Kruttika Phalnikar, M Srividya, SV Mythri, NS Vasavi, Archisha Ganguly, Aparajita Kumar, Padmaja S, Kishan Kalia, Srishti S Mishra, Sreeja Kumari Dhanya, Pradip Paul, Bharath Holla, Suhas Ganesh, Puli Chandramouli Reddy, Reeteka Sud, Biju Viswanath\*, Bhavana Muralidharan\* (\*corresponding; first five authors contributed equally)

**Institutions:** inStem (Bengaluru); NIMHANS (Bengaluru); Shiv Nadar Institution of Eminence (Delhi-NCR)

**Summary:** Cortical organoids derived from bipolar disorder (BD) patients and healthy controls from a clinically dense family in the Indian population showed neurodevelopmental anomalies. Patient organoids had fewer neuroepithelial buds/cortical rosettes, a smaller ventricular zone, and fewer SOX2-positive and EdU-positive cycling progenitors, indicating a progenitor proliferation defect. Transcriptomics showed dysregulation of genes involved in progenitor proliferation and neuronal migration, and time-lapse imaging of 2D neural stem cells showed abnormal migration in BD samples.

**Keywords:** cortical organoids, bipolar disorder, neuroepithelial bud organization, cellular migration

## 2. Gene Expression Analysis in Stem Cell-derived Cortical Neuronal Cultures Using Multi-well SYBR Green Quantitative PCR Arrays

**Bio-protocol**, 12(14): e4476, July 20, 2022 · DOI: [10.21769/BioProtoc.4476](https://doi.org/10.21769/BioProtoc.4476)

**Authors:** Vasavi Nallur Srinivasaraghavan, Faria Zafar, Birgitt Schüle\*

**Institution:** Department of Pathology, Stanford University School of Medicine

**Summary:** A customizable, medium-throughput and cost-efficient SYBR Green qPCR array (27 markers plus two housekeeping genes) for quickly screening how efficiently human iPSCs differentiate into excitatory cortical neurons. The array covers pluripotency, neuroprogenitor, cortical neuron and synaptic markers, and was used across four time points of Ngn2-driven differentiation. Pluripotency genes dropped sharply within the first three days, while neuronal and synaptic markers rose steadily.

**Keywords:** human iPSCs, cortical neurons, neuronal differentiation, SYBR Green, quantitative PCR, multi-well qPCR, primer design

This protocol was validated in publication 3 below.

## 3. Isogenic human SNCA gene dosage induced pluripotent stem cells to model Parkinson's disease

**Stem Cell Research**, 60 (2022) 102733 · DOI: [10.1016/j.scr.2022.102733](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.scr.2022.102733)

**Authors:** Faria Zafar, Vasavi Nallur Srinivasaraghavan, Max Yang Chen, C. Alejandra Morato Torres, Birgitt Schüle\*

**Institution:** Department of Pathology, Stanford University School of Medicine

**Summary:** An isogenic alpha-synuclein (*SNCA*) gene dosage model built with CRISPR/Cas9. Frameshift mutations were introduced into exon 2 of *SNCA* in iPSCs from a patient with an *SNCA* triplication, and clones with different frameshift mutations were derived and characterized. The panel lets researchers study the effects of varying alpha-synuclein expression levels.

**Keywords:** CRISPR, Cas9, alpha-synuclein, *SNCA*, gene regulation, gene editing, Parkinson's disease, developmental delay, autism spectrum disorder
